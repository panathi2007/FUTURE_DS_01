# Task 1: Business Sales Performance Analytics

##  Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing retail sales data for Global Superstore to identify key revenue drivers, high-value product categories, geographic performance, and overall sales trends over a four-year period.

##  Tools Used
* **Microsoft Excel** (Data Cleaning, Pivot Tables, and Dashboard Design)

##  Key Business Insights
* **Regional Performance:** The **West** region is the top revenue generator, contributing the highest total sales volume, followed closely by the East region.
* **Category Analysis:** **Technology** is the highest-performing product category, yielding the top sales numbers, closely followed by Furniture.
* **Sales Trends:** While sales experienced a minor dip between 2015 and 2016, the business achieved strong, continuous revenue growth through 2017 and 2018.
